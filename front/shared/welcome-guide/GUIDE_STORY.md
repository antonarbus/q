# Welcome Guide Story Plan

## Character

**John Carter** — independent electrician, 12 years experience, works solo.
Client: **Anna** — renovating her apartment, needs a new electricity distribution box installed.

---

## Slides

---

### Slide 1 — The Job

**Step label:** Meet John
**Title:** A client needs a quote. Fast.
**Description:** Anna is renovating her apartment and needs a new electricity distribution box installed. She messages John. He needs to send a professional quote today — not a PDF cobbled together in Word.

**Screenshot:** John opens the app and starts a new quotation. Shows the empty quotation with title "Electricity Distribution Box Installation — Anna's Apartment".

---

### Slide 2 — Build the Pitch

**Step label:** Write the proposal
**Title:** Tell the client why to choose you
**Description:** John writes a rich commercial description — what the job includes, why it's worth the price, a photo of a past installation. He adds a scope of supply table with line items, quantities, and unit prices.

**Screenshot:** Quotation with a filled rich-text block. Example content below.

#### Rich text block content (to type into app):

---

**Professional Electrical Distribution Box Installation**

Hi Anna,

Thank you for reaching out. Here's what I'll deliver for your apartment renovation.

I specialise in residential electrical work with over 12 years of experience. Every installation is done to code, fully documented, and backed by a 2-year workmanship guarantee.

**What's included:**

- Full replacement of the existing distribution box
- Installation of modern circuit breakers (16A, 25A, 40A)
- Labelling of all circuits
- Safety inspection and sign-off document

_Photo: completed installation in a similar apartment (85 m², 2022)_
[insert photo here]

### Slide 3 — Terms & Payment

**Step label:** Set the terms
**Title:** Delivery timeline and how to pay
**Description:** John adds his terms and conditions — a delivery schedule so Anna knows exactly when each phase happens. Then he drops in a payment block. Anna can pay a deposit directly from the quote, no back-and-forth needed.

**Screenshot:** Quotation with a terms block and payment block visible. Example content below.

#### Terms & conditions block content:

---

**Terms & Conditions**

**Payment:**

- 50% deposit required to confirm the booking
- Remaining 50% due on completion

**Timeline:**

| Phase | Description                             | Duration |
| ----- | --------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Day 1 | Materials procurement                   | 1 day    |
| Day 2 | Distribution box removal & installation | 4 hours  |
| Day 2 | Testing & circuit labelling             | 1 hour   |
| Day 3 | Safety inspection & document handover   | 1 hour   |

**Warranty:** 2 years on all workmanship. Manufacturer warranty applies to parts.

**Cancellation:** Full deposit refund if cancelled more than 48 hours before start date.

---

#### Payment block:

- Label: "Confirm your booking — pay 50% deposit"
- Amount: $325

---

### Slide 4 — Send. Get Paid.

**Step label:** Share the quote
**Title:** One link. Client pays in one click.
**Description:** John copies the share link and sends it to Anna over WhatsApp. She opens it on her phone — a clean, professional document. She reads it, likes what she sees, and pays the deposit. John gets a notification. The job is booked.

**Screenshot:** The share modal open, showing the quotation link ready to copy. Optionally a second screenshot of what the client sees (the public quotation view on mobile).

## Notes for screenshots

- Use realistic but short data — don't fill every field, keep it scannable
- Highlight the key UI element in each shot (use a subtle red circle or arrow overlay if needed)
- Crop tightly to the relevant area, not the full browser window
- Suggested size: 1200×800px or similar 3:2 ratio
- Save as: `public/welcome-guide/slide-1.png` through `slide-4.png`
