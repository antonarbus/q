Build and manage your commercial quotations

Offer consists of text, products and price blocks

  • all blocks are customizable
  • blocks can be copied, cut, deleted, inserted, sorted and resized
  • blocks can be saved in bookmarks at your profile
  • on top of the page there is a search field to find and paste saved block
  • you may attach private internal information to any block

Cover letter

  • it is the text block
  • show your technical and marketing information about your products and services
  • all text is customizable and you may style it as needed, simply select the text to see style options
  • drag & drop images and files which will remain in your account
  • embed links and videos to make your offer to stand out

Quoted products are listed inside the table, you can

  • add description
  • style text
  • add files
  • calculate item and total prices
  • mix prices with text
  • change column widths
  • copy, paste, cut, delete & sort rows
  • provide additional private information
  • save an item in profile at bookmarks
  • search and paste saved items

Total price

  • total price sums up prices above
  • multiple price blocks can be on the page

Quotation in general

  • store and share quotation as link or .pdf file
  • review, track and update quotation
  • save and search though bookmarked portfolio
  • copy and paste items from other quotations
  • all visible elements are editable and customizable
