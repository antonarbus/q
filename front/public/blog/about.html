About SendMeQuotation.today

Professional quotation management for freelancers, contractors and businesses.

What is SendMeQuotation.today?

SendMeQuotation.today is a web app for building and managing commercial quotations. Instead of using Word documents or spreadsheets, you compose quotations from structured blocks — text sections, bill-of-quantities tables, price summaries, and payment links — then share them with clients via a single URL.

Clients open the quotation in browser. No account required on their side. They can review the offer and, if you've added a payment block, pay directly through a built-in Stripe payment link.

Our Story

SendMeQuotation.today was built by a former sales manager, where commercial quotations routinely started at $100,000. At that scale, the quality and clarity of a quotation directly affects whether a deal closes — a messy Word document is not an option when the client is a multinational comparing offers from three vendors.

The same principles apply whether your quote is for $50 or $500,000: structured layout, clear pricing, no ambiguity. This app brings that discipline to anyone who sends commercial offers, without the enterprise software complexity or price tag.

Who it's For

  • Sales managers and account executives issuing complex commercial offers
  • Contractors and engineers quoting large-scale projects
  • Freelancers who send proposals to clients
  • Small and large businesses that issue commercial offers regularly
  • Anyone tired of formatting quotes in Word or Excel

Key Features

  • Block-based editor — build quotations from reusable sections: text blocks, BOQ tables (with quantity, unit price, totals), price summaries, and payment blocks
  • Shareable client link — every quotation gets a unique URL your client can open without signing up
  • PDF export — download any quotation as a PDF with one click
  • Built-in payments — connect your Stripe account and embed a payment link directly in the quotation; clients pay without leaving the page
  • Bookmarks — save frequently used items, blocks, or sections and reuse them across quotations
  • File attachments — attach supporting documents or images to any quotation

Pricing

The first 100 quotations are free, with no time limit and no credit card required. After that, a one-time payment of $99 unlocks 365 more days of unlimited quotations. Purchases stack — buying while your subscription is still active extends it from the current expiry date.

How It Compares

Most quotation tools (PandaDoc, Proposify, Better Proposals) are built for sales teams and cost $30–100 per seat per month. SendMeQuotation.today is built for people who actually write quotations — sales managers, engineers, and business owners — and who need a fast, clean tool that handles both a $50 freelance job and a $500,000 industrial project with equal precision.

The built-in Stripe payment link is a genuine differentiator — most competitors require a separate invoicing step after a quote is accepted. With SendMeQuotation.today, quote and payment happen in the same link.
