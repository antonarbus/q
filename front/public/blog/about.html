About SendMeQuotation.today

Professional quotation management for freelancers, contractors and businesses.

What is SendMeQuotation.today?

SendMeQuotation.today is a web app for building and managing commercial quotations. Instead of wrestling with Word documents or spreadsheets, you put together quotations from structured blocks: text sections, bill-of-quantities tables, price summaries, and payment links. Then you share them with clients via a single URL.

Clients open the link in their browser. No account needed on their end. They can look over the offer and, if you've added a payment block, pay directly through the built-in Stripe payment link.

Our Story

SendMeQuotation.today was built by a former sales manager who dealt with commercial quotations starting at $100,000. At that scale, a clear and well-structured quote can make or break a deal. A messy Word document doesn't cut it when your client is a multinational comparing offers from three vendors.

The same principles apply whether your quote is for $50 or $500,000: a clear layout, transparent pricing, and no room for confusion. This app brings that same discipline to anyone sending commercial offers, without the enterprise software price tag.

Who it's For

  • Sales managers and account executives issuing complex commercial offers
  • Contractors and engineers quoting large-scale projects
  • Freelancers who send proposals to clients
  • Small and large businesses that issue commercial offers regularly
  • Anyone tired of formatting quotes in Word or Excel

Key Features

  • Block-based editor: build quotations from reusable sections, including text blocks, BOQ tables (with quantity, unit price, and totals), price summaries, and payment blocks
  • Shareable client link: every quotation gets a unique URL your client can open without creating an account
  • PDF export: download any quotation as a PDF with one click
  • Built-in payments: connect your Stripe account and embed a payment link directly in the quotation so clients can pay without leaving the page
  • Bookmarks: save frequently used items, blocks, or sections and reuse them across quotations
  • File attachments: attach supporting documents or images to any quotation

How It Compares

Most quotation tools like PandaDoc, Proposify, and Better Proposals are built for sales teams and cost $30 to $100 per seat per month. SendMeQuotation.today is designed for people who actually write the quotes: sales managers, engineers, and business owners who need a fast, clean tool that works just as well for a $50 freelance job as it does for a $500,000 industrial project.

The built-in Stripe payment link sets it apart from most competitors, which require a separate invoicing step after a quote is accepted. With SendMeQuotation.today, the quote and the payment live in the same link.
