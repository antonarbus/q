About SendMeQuotation.today

Quotation management tool for freelancers, contractors and businesses.

What is SendMeQuotation.today?

SendMeQuotation.today is a web app for creating and sending commercial quotations. Instead of doing quotations with Word and Excel files, you build quotes online from blocks: text sections, bill-of-quantities tables, price summaries, and payment links. Share a link with your client and get paid.

Our Story

SendMeQuotation.today was built by a former sales engineer who spent years writing quotes, from small product offers to multi-million contractor deals with thousands of line items.

As a sales manager, your job is to get a quote document. It needs a clear layout, accurate prices, some marketing context, and it has to look good.

It also needs to be fast. Past quotes and your portfolio should be right there. Edits should be easy. Files and documents should attach without hassle. Messy Word/Excel setups are not right tools for this, but SendMeQuotation.today does.

Who it's For

  • Sales managers and account executives issuing complex commercial offers
  • Contractors and engineers quoting large-scale projects
  • Freelancers who send proposals to clients
  • Small and large businesses that issue commercial offers regularly

Key Features

  • Block-based editor: build quotations from reusable sections, including text blocks with rich formatting support, bill of quantity tables (with description, quantity, unit price, and totals), price summaries, and payment blocks
  • Shareable client link: every quotation gets a unique URL your client can open without creating an account
  • PDF export: download any quotation as a PDF with one click to store it offline or share by email
  • Built-in payments: connect your Stripe account and add a payment link directly in the quotation so clients can pay to you
  • Bookmarks: save frequently used items and reuse them across quotations
  • File attachments: attach marketing or technical documents or images to any quotation
  • Quotations history: search through your past quotations and reuse parts in your next quote.

How It Compares

Most quotation tools like PandaDoc, Proposify, and Better Proposals are built for sales teams and cost $30 to $100 per seat per month. SendMeQuotation.today is designed for individuals who actually write the quotes: sales managers, engineers, big and small business owners who need a fast, clean tool that works just as well for a $50 freelance job as it does for a multi-million industrial project.

The built-in Stripe payment link sets it apart from most competitors, which require a separate invoicing step after a quote is accepted. With SendMeQuotation.today, the quote and the payment live together.
