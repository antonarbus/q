Frequently Asked Questions

Is SendMeQuotation.today free to use?

The first 100 quotations are completely free with no credit card required. After that, a one-time payment of $99 unlocks 365 days of unlimited quotations. No recurring subscription — you only pay again when you want to extend. Multiple purchases stack: buying while your access is still active extends it from the current expiry date.

Do my clients need to create an account to view a quotation?

No. Every quotation gets a unique shareable link. Your client opens it in any browser — no sign-up or login required on their side.

Can I export quotations to PDF?

Yes. Any quotation can be downloaded as a PDF with one click.

Can clients pay directly from the quotation?

Yes. If you connect your Stripe account, you can add a Payment Block to any quotation. This embeds a Stripe payment link so the client can pay without leaving the page. The payment goes directly to your Stripe account — the platform is never in the money flow.

What are Bookmarks?

Bookmarks let you save frequently used items, blocks, or full sections and insert them into any quotation in one click. Useful for standard line items, terms and conditions paragraphs, or any content you reuse often.

What types of blocks can I add to a quotation?

  • Text block — rich text section for descriptions, cover letters, terms
  • BOQ table — bill of quantities with description, quantity, unit price, and automatic totals
  • Price summary block — displays subtotals and grand total
  • Payment block — embeds a Stripe payment link so clients can pay directly

Do I need to install anything?

No. SendMeQuotation.today is a web app — it runs in your browser. No installation or download required.

Is there a mobile app?

Not currently. The quotation editor is designed for desktop use — the interface is too complex to use comfortably on a small screen. A desktop or laptop is recommended.
