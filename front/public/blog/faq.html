Frequently Asked Questions

Is SendMeQuotation.today free to use?

Your first 100 quotations are completely free, with no credit card required. After that, a one-time payment of $99 gives you 365 more days of unlimited quotations. There's no recurring subscription; you only pay again when you want to extend. Purchases stack, so buying while your access is still active adds time from your current expiry date.

Do my clients need to create an account to view a quotation?

No. Every quotation has a unique shareable link. Your client just opens it in any browser; no sign-up or login needed on their end.

Can I export quotations to PDF?

Yes. Any quotation can be downloaded as a PDF with one click.

Can clients pay directly from the quotation?

Yes. If you connect your Stripe account, you can add a Payment Block to any quotation. This embeds a Stripe payment link right in the page, so the client can pay without going anywhere else. The money goes straight to your Stripe account; we're never in the middle of the transaction.

What are Bookmarks?

Bookmarks let you save items, blocks, or full sections and drop them into any quotation in one click. It's handy for standard line items, terms and conditions, or anything else you find yourself adding again and again.

What types of blocks can I add to a quotation?

  • Text block: a rich text section for descriptions, cover letters, or terms
  • BOQ table: a bill of quantities with description, quantity, unit price, and automatic totals
  • Price summary block: displays subtotals and the grand total
  • Payment block: embeds a Stripe payment link so clients can pay right from the quotation

Do I need to install anything?

No. SendMeQuotation.today is a web app that runs entirely in your browser. Nothing to install or download.

Is there a mobile app?

Not at the moment. The quotation editor is built for desktop use; the interface is detailed enough that a small screen makes it awkward. A laptop or desktop works best.
