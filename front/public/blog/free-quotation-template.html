What Should Be Included in a Quotation?

Every professional quotation should include these essential elements:

1. Your Business Information

  • Company name and logo
  • Business address
  • Contact details (phone, email, website)
  • Business registration number (if applicable)

2. Client Information

  • Client name or company name
  • Client address
  • Project name or reference number

3. Quotation Details

  • Unique quotation number (e.g., Q-2025-001)
  • Date of issue
  • Validity period (e.g., "Valid for 14 days")

4. Itemized List of Products/Services

Description Qty Unit Price Total
Website Design - 5 pages 1 $2,000 $2,000
Logo Design - 3 concepts 1 $500 $500
Subtotal: $2,500
Tax (10%): $250
TOTAL: $2,750

5. Terms and Conditions

  • Payment terms (e.g., "50% upfront, 50% on completion")
  • Delivery timeline
  • What's included and excluded
  • Cancellation policy
  • Warranty or guarantee (if applicable)

6. Payment Information

  • Accepted payment methods
  • Bank account details or payment links
  • Currency

Quotation Template Examples by Industry

For Contractors & Construction

Construction quotations should include:

  • Detailed scope of work
  • Materials list with specifications
  • Labor costs broken down by trade
  • Timeline with milestones
  • Permits and fees (if applicable)
  • Warranty on workmanship

Example item: "Kitchen Renovation - Remove existing cabinets, install 12 linear feet of custom oak cabinets, granite countertops, new sink and faucet - 5 days"

For Freelancers & Consultants

Service-based quotations should include:

  • Deliverables clearly defined
  • Number of revision rounds
  • Timeline for each phase
  • What client must provide (assets, content, etc.)
  • Out-of-scope items

Example item: "Content Writing - 10 blog posts (1,000 words each), SEO optimized, includes 1 revision round per post - Delivery: 2 posts per week"

For Product-Based Businesses

Product quotations should include:

  • Product specifications
  • Quantity discounts (if applicable)
  • Shipping costs and method
  • Delivery timeframe
  • Return policy

Example item: "Office Chairs - Model XYZ123, ergonomic mesh back, adjustable height, black - Qty: 20 units - Includes free delivery"

Common Mistakes in Quotation Templates

1. Being Too Vague

❌ Bad: "Marketing services - $5,000"

✅ Good: "Social media management - 20 posts/month (Instagram & Facebook), community engagement, monthly analytics report - $5,000/month"

2. Forgetting the Expiration Date

Always include: "This quotation is valid for 14 days from the date of issue."

Without it, clients can accept your quote months later when your costs have increased.

3. Unclear Payment Terms

❌ Bad: "Payment required"

✅ Good: "Payment terms: 50% deposit upon acceptance, 50% within 7 days of project completion. Accepted methods: Bank transfer, PayPal."

4. Missing Contact Information

Make it easy for clients to reach you. Include phone, email, and physical address.

5. Poor Formatting

A messy, hard-to-read quotation makes you look unprofessional. Use:

  • Clear headings and sections
  • Consistent fonts and colors
  • Tables for pricing
  • White space for readability

How to Customize Your Template

Add Your Branding

  • Include your logo at the top
  • Use your brand colors
  • Add a professional header and footer

Tailor to Your Industry

  • Construction: Add compliance certifications, insurance details
  • Creative services: Include portfolio samples or case studies
  • IT/Software: Specify hosting, support, or licensing terms

Save Common Items

If you offer the same services repeatedly, save them as reusable items. For example:

  • "Standard website hosting - $50/month"
  • "Logo design package - 3 concepts, 2 revisions - $500"
  • "Hourly consulting rate - $150/hour"

Best Practices for Using Quotation Templates

  1. Always customize - Don't send generic templates. Add client name, project details, specific deliverables.
  2. Review before sending - Check for typos, calculation errors, and outdated information.
  3. Follow up - If you don't hear back in 3-4 days, send a friendly reminder.
  4. Track everything - Keep a database of all quotations sent, accepted, and declined.
  5. Learn from rejections - If quotations are being rejected, ask for feedback.
